How Relay works

1

Nurse speaks

The outgoing nurse dictates naturally at the end of shift, on any device.

2

Relay structures it

AI converts speech into clean SBAR format instantly.

3

Flags and open loops surface

Critical risks and unresolved follow-ups get flagged automatically.

4

Next shift reads in

The incoming nurse opens Relay, sees a clean note per room, and walks in ready.
